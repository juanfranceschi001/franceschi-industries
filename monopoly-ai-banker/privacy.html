Monopoly AI Banker - Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 18, 2026

Monopoly AI Banker ("the app") is a companion app for the Monopoly board game that tracks player balances and lets you scan photos of money and property cards to update balances automatically. This page explains what data the app handles.

No account, no login

The app does not require or offer any account creation, sign-in, or user identification of any kind.

Data stored on your device

Player names and balances for your current game are stored only in local storage on your device. This data is never uploaded to, or stored on, any server we operate. Clearing the app's data or ending the game removes it.

Photos used for scanning

When you use the "scan" feature, the photo you take or choose is sent to a server we operate (a Cloudflare Worker), which forwards it to Google's Gemini API to identify money and property cards in the image. The photo is used only to generate that one response and is not stored by us afterward. As with any network request, your device's IP address is incidentally visible to Cloudflare and Google's infrastructure while processing the request. Review Google's Gemini API terms for how Google handles that data.

Advertising

The app shows banner ads through Google AdMob. AdMob may collect device identifiers and other data to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's advertising policy. We don't control what AdMob collects; you can review or reset your ad personalization settings in your device's Google settings.

No other analytics or tracking

Outside of AdMob's own ad-serving data collection described above, the app does not include analytics SDKs or other tracking technology.

Children

The app is a general-audience board game companion and is not directed at children. It does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
