About

Franceschi Industries is an independent software studio run by Juan Franceschi, building small, single-purpose apps that do one thing well. We favor simple, transparent tools over bloated platforms -- no unnecessary accounts, no unnecessary data collection.

Products

Blue Chip

An Android app for tracking stocks you own and getting simple, transparent rule-based buy/hold/sell suggestions. Runs entirely on-device, with no account or sign-in required.

Death Spotter

Ever get a strange feeling somewhere and wonder if someone died nearby? Death Spotter can check. It surfaces notable historical deaths and U.S. traffic fatality records near your location, plus community-submitted entries clearly labeled as unverified.
