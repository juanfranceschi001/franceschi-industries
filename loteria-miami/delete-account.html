Delete Your Loteria Miami Account

This page explains how to delete your account for Loteria Miami, made by Franceschi Industries, and what happens to your data.

Delete it in the app (instant)

  1. Open Loteria Miami and tap Play online.
  2. On the online menu, tap Delete account and confirm.

Or ask us to delete it

Email juan.franceschi5@gmail.com from the address on your Google account (or tell us your nickname if you played as a guest) and we will delete it within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your sign-in account (Google or guest) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Your profile: nickname, games played and games won.
  • Any game room you were in: rooms are removed when the host leaves, and your player entry is removed when you leave.

What is kept

We keep nothing about you after deletion. Ad-related data collected by Google AdMob is held by Google under its own policies; you can manage it in your Android settings under Google > Ads.

See also the Privacy Policy.
