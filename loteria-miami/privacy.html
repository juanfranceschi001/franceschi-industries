Privacy Policy for Loteria Miami

Last updated: October 2, 2026

Franceschi Industries ("we," "us," or "our") operates the Loteria Miami mobile game (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains what information the App collects, how it is used, and your choices.

Playing Without an Account

The single-player mode (playing against bots) and the card gallery work without an account and do not send any game data to us.

Online Play and Accounts

Online multiplayer requires signing in, either with a Google account or as a guest with a nickname you choose. Accounts and game data are handled by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Firebase Realtime Database). We collect and store:

  • Account identifier. A unique user ID. If you sign in with Google, Firebase also receives the email address and name on that Google account; we do not display your email to other players.
  • Display name. The nickname you choose (up to 16 characters). It is visible to other players in the same game room. Please do not use your real name or personal information as a nickname.
  • Game statistics. Your number of games played and games won.
  • Game room data. Room codes, the players in a room, their boards, marked cards and the game result. Rooms are deleted when the host leaves, and player entries are removed when a player leaves or disconnects.

We use this information only to run online games, show your name and results to other players in your room, and keep your stats. We do not sell your personal information.

Advertising

The App displays banner ads served through Google AdMob. To deliver and measure ads, Google and its partners may automatically collect information from your device, including the Android Advertising ID, device information, IP address and approximate location derived from it, and ad interaction data. In the European Economic Area and the United Kingdom, the App first shows Google's consent form (User Messaging Platform) and does not request ads until you have responded. This data is handled under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Third-Party Services

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Realtime Database) — accounts and online game data. See Firebase privacy information.
  • Google Sign-In — optional sign-in with a Google account.
  • Google AdMob — advertising.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete your account and your profile data (name and stats) at any time in the App: open Play online, then choose Delete account. You can also email us at the address below to request deletion. Signing out does not delete your data.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can delete it.

Your Choices

You can opt out of personalized advertising on Android under Settings > Google > Ads, and reset your advertising identifier there at any time. You can play against bots without creating an account.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date.
